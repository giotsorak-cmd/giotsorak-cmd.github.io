Panoramic View Apartment

Thessaloniki, next to the International Fair

About this stay

up to 6 guests · 2 bedrooms · 2 beds · 1 sofa bed · 1 bathroom · 60 m²

A bright, fully renovated 60 m² apartment with a panoramic view over the whole of Thessaloniki, from the White Tower to Seich Sou forest, and the sea on the horizon.

Open living and dining room with wooden floors, an electric fireplace and a large TV. The sofa opens into a bed.

Two bedrooms with TV and air conditioning: the first with a 160×200 double bed, a desk and access to the balcony, the second with a 140×200 double bed.

Fully equipped kitchen with oven, ceramic hob, fridge, microwave, Nespresso machine, kettle and toaster, plus a breakfast bar.

A modern bathroom with a rain shower, towels, bathrobes and toiletries.

The balcony, with a small table and chairs, looks out over the city: perfect for morning coffee or a glass of wine in the evening.
