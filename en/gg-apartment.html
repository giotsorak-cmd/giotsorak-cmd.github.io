About this stay

up to 6 guests · 2 bedrooms · 2 beds · 1 bathroom · 78 m²

A modern, fully renovated 78 m² apartment on Vasilissis Olgas Avenue, a few minutes from the seafront, with restaurants, bakeries and supermarkets all around.

A large living room with a very large corner sofa, big enough to sleep on, and a TV. Open, fully equipped kitchen with dining table, oven, hob, fridge, microwave and washing machine.

Two bedrooms with double beds and air conditioning, plus a work corner with a desk.

A modern bathroom with a glass shower, towels, bathrobes and toiletries.

The balcony, with an awning, a small table and armchairs, looks out over the city.
