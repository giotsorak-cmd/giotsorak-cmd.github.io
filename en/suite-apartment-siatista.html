Suite Apartment Siatista

Siatista, Kozani

About this stay

up to 6 guests · 2 bedrooms · 2 beds · 1 sofa bed · 1 bathroom · 60 m²

A modern, carefully finished apartment with two bedrooms and a large private terrace in Siatista.

Open living room with a corner sofa that opens into a bed, a dining table and a large TV. The kitchen is fully equipped, with an oven, hob, fridge, microwave and a Nespresso machine.

The first bedroom has a double bed with a wooden headboard, a desk, a TV and a door to the terrace. The second has a double bed, a TV and colour-changing bedside lamps.

A modern bathroom with a shower column, a wooden vanity and a backlit mirror, with towels and toiletries.

The large terrace, with a table and chairs, is perfect for your morning coffee. Also available: washing machine, iron, hair dryer and a safe.
